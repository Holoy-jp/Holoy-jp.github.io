## Research Interests

My research is in analytic number theory, with particular interests in automorphic forms and L-functions, multiplicative functions, and probabilistic models of multiplicative functions. I study the oscillation and cancellation of arithmetic functions on sparse sets, including polynomial sequences and Beatty sequences.

My work uses Dirichlet-series methods, exponential-sum estimates, second-moment methods, sieve methods, and the circle method. I also use probabilistic multiplicative models, including the Rademacher and Steinhaus models, to investigate sign changes and random-like behaviour.
