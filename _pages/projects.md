My research interests lie in analytic number theory, particularly in the following areas.

### Automorphic forms and L-functions

Sums of automorphic coefficients, symmetric power L-functions, and questions involving the distribution of zeros of L-functions.

### Multiplicative functions in short intervals

The distribution and cancellation of multiplicative functions, with emphasis on mean values and short-interval estimates.

### Prime numbers and sieve methods

Prime numbers in sparse sequences, polynomial representations of primes, and applications of sieve methods and exponential sums.

For published and accepted papers, see [Publications](/publications/).
