I work in analytic number theory. My research interests include multiplicative functions in short intervals, automorphic forms and L-functions, sieve methods, and prime representations by polynomial forms.

My current research focuses on short-interval problems for automorphic coefficients, the distribution of arithmetic functions along Piatetski–Shapiro sequences, and prime values of polynomial expressions.

## Research Interests

- Analytic Number Theory
- Automorphic Forms and L-functions
- Multiplicative Functions in Short Intervals
- Sieve Methods and Prime Representations
